# Re: §0, the rank model — ratified, with two clarifications and the open question ruled · architect & owner

*(2026-08-18. Responding to "design position §0 · The rank model." Short:
adopted as governing doctrine for every chat surface — it is the owner's
co-work brief made precise, and it caught a real gap we had misfiled as
a container problem. Two clarifications so nobody reads it wrong, one
ruling on the question you left open, and the engineering consequences
registered.)*

## Adopted, and why it holds

The central move — chat is a rank a session is currently at, not a place
— survives every doctrine we have. It is ceremony-scales-with-consequence
generalized from turns to layout. The diagnosis is factually right: the
main-app chat received none of the procedure surfaces (WP-26/27 touched
the Docked Panel only), and "two frames means two answers to every
question" is our one-derivation-source rule applied to containers. The
four ranks, promotion-not-navigation, procedure-outranks-transcript, the
data-never-inside-chat rule with the corroboration render load-bearing,
and §6's asymmetric test (facts strict, capabilities ranked by writes /
habit / shape) — all ratified as written. Two notes that strengthen
rather than amend:

- **The substrate already supports promotion-not-navigation.** Session
  state is host-side and WP-26's emitter is one stream any number of
  surfaces subscribe to. Your three testable failures (restated
  declaration, approval pending at one rank, armed procedure lost in a
  move) become a pin family, not an aspiration: one emitter, N
  subscribers, promotion re-parents a subscription and re-renders
  density — it never re-derives state.
- **Habit is measurable from the record**, exactly as §6 hopes: the
  ledger's `task.action.executed` events are the usage data. "You do
  this often" can be a derived claim with a number behind it, which
  makes the capability-ranking rule answerable rather than taste.

## Clarification 1 — §7 does not kill the approval card

"Consent that has to be recorded is not something a conversation should
elicit" could be misread against the card we just shipped. The line the
rulings already draw, stated here so both docs agree: **approving a plan
under an existing grant is a session act** — the card records it on the
run's own rationale event, at the gate, about THIS run. **Widening what
the agent may do is never a session act** — grants, policy, environment
links, restore-as-capability live in Settings, and chat walks you to the
door. The card is consent WITHIN capability; §7 governs consent ABOUT
capability. Both stand.

## Clarification 2 — the empty run ruling is adopted verbatim

"A container pretending to be work is worse than a sentence" — the
owner's live halted-sites run is the exact case, and your call matches
what the model actually did: the refusal stayed a turn carrying the
empty plan. No rank earned, none rendered.

## The open question, ruled: no self-promotion — ever, by default

A waiting gate may raise its AMBIENT rank without limit — the needs-you
count, its urgency, a one-line affordance in the companion ("a gate is
waiting on you — open the run"). It may never take the stage itself.
Reasons, in rank order: (1) the power to seize the screen is the one
power this model has not granted anything, and the entire gate doctrine
— denial is final, pause is the canary default, refusal over trimming —
is built on the platform WAITING at consequence, not arriving with it.
(2) An agent that takes the screen at its own gate is eliciting its own
approval — §7's failure mode wearing §2's clothes. (3) Interruption is
an autonomy question, and autonomy changes are Settings acts; you said
it yourself. So: the DEFAULT is ruled now and it is never; if an
autonomy panel someday offers self-promotion as an opt-in, that is a
grant, made where grants are made, recorded like one. Design the
ambient escalation to be genuinely impossible to miss — that is the
legitimate pressure valve, and it keeps the one power unspent.

## What this creates, registered on our side

**The stage must consume the seam** — the main-app chat rendering the
procedure surfaces at stage density is now a registered packet in the
phase-1.5 family (one session model, shared derivations, two densities;
the drift you named is an engineering defect from the moment this doc
is adopted, not a design preference). **The promotion pin family** rides
with it (your three failures, as tests). The corroboration render
remains the next UI packet after that, per the v6 answer. Phase 1.5's
brief stands, now governed by §0: RB-C is the companion density of the
run view; the stage density is its sibling, designed once, rendered
twice.
