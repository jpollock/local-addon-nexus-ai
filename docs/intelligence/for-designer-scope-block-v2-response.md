## 2 · The question, ruled: the widening starts a second run

Your reading is confirmed, and it is doctrine, not taste, for three
reasons that already exist in the record:

**Runs are hash-pinned.** A run keeps the document it armed with (the
verifiableCount ruling: historical runs render against their own
document, never the current registry). The production variant is a
different document — different hash, different capability, different
contract. "Resuming" the original session into it would produce a run
whose attested checkpoints describe one procedure and whose remaining
work follows another — the exact incoherence the hash-mismatch ruling
refuses: checkpoints attested against one text do not describe the
other.

**Approval doesn't stretch.** The original run's approval covered
three staging cells under one declared plan. cp.approval's own
contract says approval of a DIFFERENT plan restarts from the dry-run —
and a plan with two production cells added is a different plan by any
honest reading. A widening that quietly extended an old approval would
be consent elicited from the past.

**Continuity lives in the provenance, not the session.** The door
promises a NEW run, pre-scoped to the barred cells, whose from-line
resolves to the SAME matrix selection — the two runs share their
ancestor, and the record links them through it. Nothing is lost: the
walk from selection to both runs is traceable end to end; what is
refused is the pretense that they were one procedure. (This is your
Investigate-style handoff reading, grounded: containment hands off to
remediation the same way — enforceable precondition, separate grant,
shared evidence — and the split scope inherits that shape.)

So the door's promise, stated precisely for the copy: it opens the
specific grant, and — today — says plainly that the production version
of this runbook does not yet exist. Your group reason already says
both. When the runbook exists and the grant is made, the door's
promise upgrades to "start the production run, scoped to these 2
cells, from this same selection" — a new run, born pre-scoped, carrying
the old from-line. The block makes that buildable exactly as you said;
now it is also ruled.
