## 1 · The badge: your reading is ruled, with one honesty line added

A deferral lowers escalation, and the badge IS escalation — the
ambient channel is precisely the thing the user asked to quiet, so a
badge that kept counting a deferred situation would mean the deferral
deferred nothing. The badge does not "undercount on purpose"; it
counts exactly what it claims — situations currently escalating — and
the framing to keep is that the badge is an instrument, not an
inventory. The inventory is the list, where the deferred row stands
at full tier with its reason. One addition, pinned: the panel's own
accounting line states the deferral, so the count can never read as
the whole truth — "1 needs you · 1 deferred by you" in the subhead.
The colour-only alternative is refused: a red badge over a quieted
situation is escalation continuing under a different name.

## 2 · "Wake" is not a new word — you are quoting the register

XD-13's ratified text already says "deferral = session act,
user-only, wake conditions" — the word entered the record at §2's
ratification, in your own document. So there is nothing to mint, only
a row to add, and it is added: Controlled Vocabulary v1.2 now carries
**deferred** ("deferred by you until <condition>", reason on the
row — never *snoozed*, *muted*, *dismissed*), **wake** ("wakes when
<condition>" — never *expires*, because a wake can be an event, not
only a date; your sentinel-scan wake option is why), and **end the
deferral** (never *cancel*). Render as you drew it.

## 3 · Ending early is a session act, and it is recorded

Your assumption is ruled correct, by symmetry that is already
doctrine: the deferral is a recorded statement about this run at this
moment, and ending it early is a second statement that supersedes the
first — recorded the same way, superseding never mutating (the
resolution pattern from the incident producer, applied to the
deferral's own lifecycle). Three ways a deferral ends, all on the
record: the wake condition fires (the record names which), the user
ends it early (recorded), or the situation resolves (the row leaves
the list by being answered, which is the only exit the sheet
permits). Nothing the agent does can end a deferral any more than
start one. The row's affordance is honest as drawn.
