# Design protocol — how the design workstream runs

*(2026-08-18. PARALLEL_PROTOCOL's sibling: the codification of what the
designer loop already practices, written down so the method survives a
second designer the way the agent protocol survives a new agent.
Companion to `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` (the register) and the UX workstream
section of `INTELLIGENCE_ROADMAP.md`.)*

## The loop

Work moves in POSITIONS and RESPONSES, both written, both committed.
A designer position (§N) proposes; an architect response ratifies,
amends with reasons, or refuses with reasons; what survives enters the
register (XD-numbers) and, where load-bearing, becomes a pin or a
fixture. Chat between humans is fine; decisions that live only in chat
bind nobody (the agent protocol's rule, unchanged).

## Per sheet (the packet DoD's sibling)

1. **Names its moment** — which moment(s) it serves; a sheet serving
   no moment invokes the complexity budget (XD-4).
2. **Names its fixture source** — procedure data comes from the shared
   derived fixture file (XD-16), never transcribed; scenario data is
   labeled fictional; a fixture that contradicts shipped law is either
   the point (design the refusal state) or a defect — say which.
3. **Ships its pins** — testable assertions, must-nots first-class
   (the never-shows with teeth, XD-19). A sheet without pins is a
   sketch, welcome but unratifiable.
4. **Conforms to the laws** — vocabulary (XD-1), derived-never-authored
   (XD-2) including in fixture prose, rank (XD-3), moment contents
   (XD-4). Attest words in full; no verified ticks (XD-10).
5. **States what is deliberately absent** — the absences list is part
   of the sheet, so a reviewer can tell restraint from omission.

## Ratification

Responses answer BY NUMBER. A ratified pin becomes an acceptance
criterion of the packet that builds the surface (scope block → WP-32
is the template). An amendment is credited inline in the amended doc.
A decline with better reasoning than the proposal is ratified as the
rule (the shortform-marks precedent). Superseded sheets are marked in
their own headers and never updated (XD-17).

## Design sittings (the judged halves)

Each journey's judged question is answered by a real person against
real surfaces, on the record: the script is the journey spec, the
subject's answers are recorded in WORK_PACKETS like any sitting, and
pass³ discipline applies where the journey gates a write. The first
sitting of any shipped surface doubles as its live smoke. Fabricated
verdicts are forbidden the way faked LLM verdicts are: a judged
criterion nobody sat renders OWNER-PENDING, never pass.

## Instrumentation

The walks fold (registered) measures transitions, not states: a moment
whose inbound walks never fire is ours, not the user's; a frequent
walk missing from the closed list is a candidate moment announcing
itself (XD-4). The consequence order is pinned by the one-morning
fixture (XD-13); changes to the order re-rule the fixture's expected
output, never just the prose.

## What this protocol refuses

New vocabulary by drift (propose a row, ratify it). Fixtures that
teach what shipped law refuses — unless designing the refusal is the
point. A checkpoint sequence authored anywhere (XD-16). Two wordings
of one seam sentence. Rulings ratified on the strength of one
person's morning — the fixture is the argument's durable form.
