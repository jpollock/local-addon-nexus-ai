## 0 · Adjudication of the sheet

Ratified whole. The three states carry exactly ADR-24's semantics,
the tally line is derived from the spans it counts, and two lines are
worth quoting back as the doctrine's best statements yet: "a claim
pointing at a record nobody supplied is worse than a claim pointing
at nothing: it looks like evidence" — that is why unresolvable is the
loudest state, said better than the ADR says it — and the legacy
card's reasoning, "an old session must not render as though every
sentence chose to go uncited." The absences are all load-bearing;
no-footnote-list ("a bibliography moves the evidence away from the
sentence that needs it") and no-copy-of-record-contents ("a copy is a
second place a fact can be wrong") both enter the register inside
XD-24. One standing note, the swap pattern's third application: the
scenario file's citation ids and states are fictional today because
no convention exists to derive from; when WP-34 fixes the syntax and
its adherence fixtures exist, this sheet's scenario swaps onto
generated data, same as the procedure sheets did.

## 1 · The Chip stays; a mono variant needs evidence, not taste

Keep the system's Chip in the system's face. This is the
badge-loudness ruling applied again: no off-system overrides at the
exact spot where trust is the subject. The mono-for-identifiers guide
is a convention of our documents, not yet a law of markers, and an id
of six glyphs at xs size is not where mono earns its keep. If a
sitting or the adherence eval shows ids being misread or chip text
being mistaken for prose, that finding is the DS variant proposal's
evidence — same instrument, same loop, as the badge.

## 2 · Trailing position is ratified as the render rule; the syntax is WP-34's, and now carries a consuming contract

Your theory is adopted as the pin: a claim's source belongs at the
end of the claim and nowhere else — a door where the sentence ends,
never a superscript the eye must chase, never a footnote. The
emitted SYNTAX remains the packet's to fix (P2), but this
ratification flows a constraint back into WP-34: whatever the model
emits must make the record id (and the citation kind) addressable so
the trailing chip can render it — the convention is now answerable
to a consumer, which is exactly how the payload contract survived
its own gate. The anti-superscript position is adopted with it.

## 3 · The trust words: keep what you drew, because you derived it

"Supplied to this task by the ledger" is not an invented vocabulary —
it derives from the manifest's supplied relationship, and "supplied"
is already ruled (XD-7: supplied vs quoted). So the chip renders the
derived supply sentence, as drawn. The carrier's machine trust
classes (`emitted`, `told`, and their kin) are envelope words that
have never faced a user; the day two trust classes must be visibly
DIFFERENT on this surface — a told record beside an emitted one —
that difference is a vocabulary row to propose, and you will have the
distinction's first real specimen to design from. Until then, no
bare machine words on the chip and no invented softening either.

## 4 · Tool-call citations stay technical, and the precedent already says why

`wpe_backup_and_verify #2` is ratified as drawn. Honest-and-technical
beats warm-and-authored — the door-label ruling, applied verbatim: a
human phrase for a tool call would be authoring at the exact place
the surface exists to prove it doesn't author. It reads like a
machine because it is a machine's testimony, and the trace is the
only party entitled to rename it.
