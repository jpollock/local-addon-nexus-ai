## 1 · The worked walk — one story, seven moments

Tuesday morning. A WooCommerce update exists for five staging sites.
This is the whole product, in order:

**Glance.** She opens Local. The ambient rank says: `2 need you · all
producers reporting`. One needs-you row reads "WooCommerce 9.5.0
available on 5 sites · 2 of them have history." She has read eleven
words and knows everything a glance owes her.
*Handoff artifact: the needs-you row IS the door — clicking it opens
Inspect with the five sites already the subject.*

**Inspect.** The matrix, filtered to the five: one row per fact, one
column per place. The WooCommerce row disagrees with NEWEST everywhere;
Bravo and Charlie's rows carry the history badge ("checkout broke on a
WooCommerce update, 30d ago"). No prose asserts anything a cell doesn't
show.
*Handoff artifact: the behind-set — she selects it, and "update these"
carries the five site ids into the act-big as its scope. She never
retypes what she just looked at.*

**Act-big.** The session arms `rb.bulk-plugin-update`. At companion
rank the declared block owns the column: eight checkpoints, four
provable, badges only on the added prudence. The dry-run diff shows
five rows and one skip (Foxtrot, halted, said so). The card arrives
with the derived line — the platform can't verify the dry-run was
shown; read the plan above — and the canary choice, pause as default.
She approves; backups attest per site; Alpha updates alone.
*Handoff artifact: the run itself, promotable to stage without
restating one word (the promotion pins).*

**The abort.** Charlie's verify fails checkout. The run halts AT
Charlie: the abort panel shows four honest groups — 2 done and
standing (backup ids attached), 1 failed with its evidence, 0 skipped,
2 untouched, and the lead line counts rather than asserts. "Restore
this site" sits on Charlie's row as a launcher.
*Handoff artifact: the abort's evidence — site, version pair (once its
producer lands), the failing check — travels whole into Investigate.*

**Investigate.** Stage rank; conversational by nature. The evidence
chain renders claims-linked-to-records: the incident event, the
correlation with payment-gateway-x, what changed just before. Every
sentence has a door; an unlinked claim would be visually loud. The fix
identified, the agent names the act it licenses — remediation, its own
runbook, its own grant.
*Handoff artifact: the confirmed cause becomes the next act-big's
justification, cited in ITS dry-run.*

**Return.** She leaves. Next morning the ambient rank says "1 waiting
on you — the halted run at Charlie." Opening it resumes the SAME
session at the same gate — nothing re-rendered, nothing re-asked. The
finished portion is already filed at Record rank, procedure beside
run, for the client report.
*Handoff artifact: the wait itself — the needs-you row knows WHERE in
the run she is needed, not just that she is.*

**Govern.** The remediation runbook needs a capability she has never
granted. The refusal names the missing grant and the door; Settings
shows the matrix, the grant is made, visible, revocable — recorded as
a control event, never elicited in the chat that walked her there.
*Handoff artifact: the refusal's name for what was missing IS the
deep-link into Govern.*

**The test of the whole walk:** she stated her intent once (at the
matrix), approved once (at the card), and never re-established context.
Every transition carried its artifact. That is co-work; a chatbox is
what you get when any one of those handoffs drops.

## 4 · Journey-eval skeletons — to finish together

The B-03 discipline: criteria BEFORE surfaces, programmatic half as
pins, judged half as sittings. Skeletons only — the must/must-nots need
your eye before they bind anyone:

**J-Glance** · cold open → "anything wrong?" answered.
Key steps: verdict visible without interaction; every count derived;
one door per fact. Must-nots: a question asked; a stale fact undated;
ceremony of any kind. (Programmatic: derivation pins, route-exists.
Judged: can a person say what needs them in ≤5 seconds?)

**J-Inspect** · a named divergence → its explanation → a scoped intent.
Key steps: the shape first; the disagreeing cell explains itself
(lineage); selection becomes scope without retyping. Must-nots: prose
instead of the shape; a comparator verdict replaced by cell-inequality;
a dead-end fact (no door).

**J-Act-small** · one change on one site, motivated by one fact.
Key steps: the motivating fact linked; the gate full-ceremony; the
history flag if the component has bitten. Must-nots: canary/campaign
machinery; a second write with less ceremony than the first.

**J-Return** · away during a halt → resumed at the gate.
Key steps: the wait names its gate; promotion resumes the same session
(nothing re-asked); the finished part already filed. Must-nots: a
scrollback as the re-entry; an approval that must be re-given; a
needs-you row that knows THAT but not WHERE.
