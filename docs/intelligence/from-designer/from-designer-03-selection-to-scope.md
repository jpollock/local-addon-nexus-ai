# Selection becomes scope

*The handoff between Inspect and Act-big, designed as one artifact that renders in both moments. The user recognizes their selection at the head of the declaration because it is the same block, not a description of it.*

## 1 · Inspect · the comparator render

**The behind-set is selected in the shape, not typed.** Rows are sites, columns are places, cells are the fact. Selection happens on disagreeing cells, so the scope is made of the cells that motivated it.

The specimen renders a WooCommerce row across six sites (newest 9.5.0, checked 4m ago), one behind cell per site, with history notes on the two sites where a WooCommerce update has broken checkout before. The selection bar states its unit: 5 sites, 5 cells behind newest · 1 excluded.

## 2 · The artifact that crosses — the scope block

Four derived lines and nothing else: the set, the places it spans, what it excludes and why, and where it came from. It renders identically in the selection bar and at the head of the declaration — same block, same wording, same doors.

- **Targets** — Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo — the cells behind newest
- **Places** — Touches production on 2 of 5, staging on 3 of 5
- **Excludes** — Foxtrot — halted 14h, and said so
- **From** — Your matrix selection, 12:04 · WooCommerce row

Place is rendered as the set it is, from the same derivation the ambient order sorts by. The exclusion is stated rather than silent — a target dropped without a reason is a scope the user cannot check.

## 3 · Act-big · companion density, 380px

**The declaration opens with the block she just made.** No turn restates the scope in prose. The dry-run's targets are the selected ids as a set, so there is nothing to confirm and nothing to retype — the only new thing on screen is the procedure.

### What is deliberately absent

- No turn that restates the five site names in prose.
- No confirm-your-targets step — the set is the one she selected, and re-asking would say the platform lost it.
- No place named as a single value anywhere in the block.
- No silent exclusion: Foxtrot appears in the dry run carrying its reason.

At stage density the same block sits at the head of the run document and the transcript keeps the margin. One rendering, two densities; any wording difference between them is a defect.

## The pins this specifies · J-Inspect

- The dry run's target ids equal the selected cell ids, asserted as a set, in both densities.
- The scope block's rendered text is byte-identical between the selection bar, the companion declaration, and the stage document.
- Every scope line is derived: targets from the selection, places from the affected-set derivation the ambient order sorts by, exclusions from the record that caused them.
- The from-line resolves to the comparator render that produced the selection, with the same filter applied.
- No procedure event precedes the arming, and the arming carries the scope — a run that derives its own targets fails this journey.
